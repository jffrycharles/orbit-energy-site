FAQs
A few things to know.

What does Orbit Energy do?

Orbit Energy Legal Consultants, P.A. advises on legal matters in energy, construction and infrastructure, including permitting, regulatory compliance, transactions and project development.

Which solar projects do you support?

We advise residential solar developers and purchasers, as well as commercial and industrial solar projects. Support includes contracts, site control, power purchase agreements, leases, construction, operations and maintenance, and engineering procurement, depending on the practice area.

Can you help with battery storage and backup power?

We advise on energy storage agreements, interconnection requirements and incentive eligibility for battery storage and backup power projects.

Do you support commercial wind installations?

Yes. Our wind practice covers site control, power purchase agreements, leases, construction, operations and maintenance, and engineering procurement.

What support is available for construction and contracts?

We advise on contractor licensing, project agreements, compliance and risk mitigation. We also draft and review contracts throughout energy and infrastructure development.
