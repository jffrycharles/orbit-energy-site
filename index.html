FAQs
A few things to know.

What does Orbit Energy do?

Orbit Energy Legal Consultants, P.A. provides legal consulting for renewable energy and related commercial work, including contracts, construction, risk management, technology and SaaS.

Which types of projects do you support?

Our focus ranges from residential solar panels to commercial construction. Contact us to discuss whether your project fits the practice.

Can you help with contracts and negotiation?

Contract drafting and negotiation are among our areas of focus, alongside indemnification, risk management and corporate governance.

Do you work with technology and SaaS businesses?

Information technology and SaaS are included in Orbit’s areas of focus. Get in touch to discuss the agreement or commercial question you have in mind.

How do I start a conversation?

Call the office or email Joshua Nathan with a brief outline of your enquiry. Please wait for guidance before sending confidential documents.

Not sure where to start?

Tell us what you’re working on. A conversation is the first step to understanding how we can help.
